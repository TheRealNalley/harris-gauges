Lake Corpus Christi is a water-supply lake, run by the City of Corpus Christi. It is normally held near a steady level, rising after heavy rain and falling in dry spells.

Elevation is the height of the lake surface above NGVD29 (a fixed survey reference), not a water depth. Storage is USGS's estimate of the water held, in acre-feet (one acre-foot covers an acre a foot deep).
