Lake Texana is a water-supply lake, run by the Lavaca-Navidad River Authority. It is normally held near a steady level, rising after heavy rain and falling in dry spells.

Elevation is the height of the lake surface above NAVD88 (a fixed survey reference), not a water depth. USGS publishes no storage figure for this lake.
