B. A. Steinhagen Lake sits where the Angelina and Neches rivers meet, just below Sam Rayburn Reservoir, behind Town Bluff Dam, run by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. It is normally held near a steady level, rising after heavy rain and falling in dry spells.

Elevation is the height of the lake surface above NGVD29 (a fixed survey reference), not a water depth. USGS publishes no storage figure for this lake.

Percent full is the Texas Water Development Board's measure: the water in the lake's conservation pool (the part kept for water supply, above any dead pool that can't be drawn) as a share of what that pool holds when full. It stops at 100%: water above the conservation pool is in flood storage. The figures are daily estimates worked out from the lake's latest survey, so they can differ a little from USGS's storage figure. TWDB's page for B. A. Steinhagen Lake has its full record.
