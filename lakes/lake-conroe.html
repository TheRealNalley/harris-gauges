Lake Conroe is a water-supply lake, run by the San Jacinto River Authority. It is normally held near a steady level, rising after heavy rain and falling in dry spells.

Elevation is the height of the lake surface above NGVD29 (a fixed survey reference), not a water depth. Storage is USGS's estimate of the water held, in acre-feet (one acre-foot covers an acre a foot deep).

No National Weather Service alert covering Harris County was active when this page was generated (Oct 24, 2026 at 7:20 AM CDT). Alerts are issued and expire at any time; the current list is at alerts.weather.gov.
