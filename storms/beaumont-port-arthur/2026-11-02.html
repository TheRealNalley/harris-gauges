How this event is defined

By the National Weather Service, not by this site. Every flood alert naming any of Hardin, Jasper, Jefferson, Newton and Orange counties counts, and since October 24, 2026 so does every coastal flood, storm surge, tropical storm or hurricane watch, warning or advisory; alerts that start within 24 hours of the previous one ending are one event. Each gauge's peak is its highest reading from 12 hours before the first alert to 48 hours after the last one ended: Nov 2, 2026 at 5:41 AM CST to Nov 5, 2026 at 6:00 AM CST. Large rivers crest a day or more after the rain, which is why the window runs past the alerts.
