Why this site exists

Harris County publishes these gauges on an interactive map, which answers the question "what is the level right now" and then forgets. The county feed serves only each sensor's current value, so there is no public record of what a gauge read last Tuesday. This site records every reading as it is published and keeps it.

Source. Stage readings are republished from the Harris County Flood Warning System. Flood watches, advisories and warnings are issued by NWS Houston/Galveston. This site is not affiliated with either agency.

No guidance, no forecast. This page reports measurements and official alert text only. It does not predict water levels, assess whether any road, property or route is passable, or advise any action. For decisions about safety, use official sources and local emergency management.
