The approaches to the Westhoff/Cheapside road bridge are under five to six feet of water and the bridge floor is flooded. Livestock are cut off and potentially drowned in the flood plain.

How to read this gauge

Gage height is the height of the water surface above this gauge's own zero point, which sits at 177.87 ft NAVD88. It is not a water depth and not an elevation: add 177.87 ft to get the water surface elevation.

The flood stages are the National Weather Service's, on the same scale as the gage height; they are the NWS's references for this location, not estimates made here.
