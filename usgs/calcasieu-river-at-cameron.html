How to read this gauge

Gage height is the height of the water surface above this gauge's own zero point, which sits at -3.53 ft NAVD88. It is not a water depth and not an elevation: add -3.53 ft to get the water surface elevation.

Salinity is the dissolved salt in the water, in parts per thousand (ppt); open seawater is about 35 ppt and river water close to 0. Specific conductance is how readily the water carries an electric current, adjusted to 25 °C, in microsiemens per centimeter; it rises with dissolved salts. Fresh river water is usually a few hundred µS/cm and seawater about 50,000. Provisional readings from USGS, which can be revised.
