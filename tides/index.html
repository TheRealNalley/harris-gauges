Water level is NOAA's six-minute observation: the tide plus any storm surge or wind set-up. 0 ft is mean higher high water, an ordinary high tide. Thresholds are the NWS's coastal flood levels where published, otherwise NOAA's high tide flooding levels ("HTF"). A few river stations publish no tidal datum and are shown on their own scale.
