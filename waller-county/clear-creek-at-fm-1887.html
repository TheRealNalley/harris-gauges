How to read this gauge

The number above is an elevation, not a water depth. It is the height of the water surface above a fixed reference level, so FM 1887 reads around 107 ft even when the channel is low. What matters is the distance to the top of bank: the elevation at which water reaches the top of the channel at this location. For this gauge that is 149.0 ft.

The bottom of the channel here sits at 105.0 ft, so the channel is roughly 44.0 ft deep at this crossing. Waller County publishes the top-of-bank figure with each reading; it is their reference, not an estimate made here.
