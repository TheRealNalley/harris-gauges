How to read this gauge

The number above is an elevation, not a water depth. It is the height of the water surface above a fixed reference level, so US 90 reads around 141 ft even when the channel is low. What matters is the distance to the top of bank: the elevation at which water reaches the top of the channel at this location. For this gauge that is 155.5 ft.

The bottom of the channel here sits at 140.0 ft, so the channel is roughly 15.5 ft deep at this crossing. Waller County publishes the top-of-bank figure with each reading; it is their reference, not an estimate made here.
