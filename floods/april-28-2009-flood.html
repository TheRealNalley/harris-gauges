How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between April 26, 2009 and May 2, 2009 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS, NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's gauges are the peaks the county lists for this flood on each gauge's own page, and the rain its rain gauges recorded, from harriscountyfws.org.
