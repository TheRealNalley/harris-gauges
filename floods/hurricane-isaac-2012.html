Hurricane Isaac (2012): peak water levels

90 river and stream gauges and 8 tide gauges · Baton Rouge, Houma–Thibodaux, New Orleans and the Northshore, Mississippi Gulf Coast · August 27, 2012 to September 4, 2012

Against today's NWS flood stages, 26 of the 90 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (11 at major, 4 at moderate, 11 at minor). For 11 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Wolf River near Landon, East Hobolochitto Creek near Caesar, Amite River at Highway 22 near Maurepas, Escatawpa River at I-10 near Orange Grove, Mississippi Sound at USGS Merrill Shell Bank Light, Black Bayou at Highway 621 near Prairieville and 5 more. The highest tide gauge reading was 9.51 ft above MHHW at Shell Beach (Aug 29, 2012 at 12:36 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 27, 2012 and September 4, 2012 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Baton Rouge, Houma–Thibodaux, New Orleans and the Northshore, Mississippi Gulf Coast. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
