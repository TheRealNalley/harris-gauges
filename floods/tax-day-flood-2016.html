Tax Day Flood (2016): peak water levels

76 river and stream gauges and 8 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston · April 17, 2016 to April 23, 2016

Against today's NWS flood stages, 25 of the 76 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (12 at major, 6 at moderate, 7 at minor). For 6 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Bear Creek near Barker, Langham Creek at W Little York Rd near Addicks, Greens Bayou near US Highway 75 near Houston, Little Cypress Creek near Cypress, Panther Branch at Gosling Rd, The Woodlands, Greens Bayou at Cutten Rd near Houston. The highest tide gauge reading was 4.82 ft above MHHW at Manchester (Apr 18, 2016 at 8:30 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between April 17, 2016 and April 23, 2016 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026.
