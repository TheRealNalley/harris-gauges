How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between October 8, 2018 and October 14, 2018 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Pensacola and the Emerald Coast, Panama City, Apalachicola and Tallahassee. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
