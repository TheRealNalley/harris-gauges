Hurricane Sally (2020): peak water levels

47 river and stream gauges and 11 tide gauges · Mobile Bay, Pensacola and the Emerald Coast, Panama City · September 14, 2020 to September 24, 2020

Against today's NWS flood stages, 19 of the 47 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (5 at major, 6 at moderate, 8 at minor). For 7 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Shoal River near Mossy Head, Styx River near Loxley at Smiths Siding, Bruce Creek at SH 81 near Redbay, Yellow River near Oak Grove, Yellow River near Milton, Styx River at Seminole and 1 more. The highest tide gauge reading was 5.60 ft above MHHW at Pensacola (Sep 16, 2020 at 7:36 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between September 14, 2020 and September 24, 2020 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Mobile Bay, Pensacola and the Emerald Coast, Panama City. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
