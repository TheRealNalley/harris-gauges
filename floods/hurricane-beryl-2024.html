Hurricane Beryl (2024): peak water levels

177 river and stream gauges and 20 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Victoria and Matagorda Bay · July 7, 2024 to July 12, 2024

Against today's NWS flood stages, 16 of the 177 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (2 at major, 2 at moderate, 12 at minor). For 4 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Cole Creek at Deihl Rd, Houston, Oyster Creek at CR 42 near Rosharon, Oyster Creek at CR 34 near Angleton, Oyster Creek at CR 290 near Angleton. The highest tide gauge reading was 8.82 ft above MHHW at Manchester (Jul 8, 2024 at 10:30 AM CDT).
