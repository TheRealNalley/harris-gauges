August 2016 Louisiana Flood: peak water levels

107 river and stream gauges and 13 tide gauges · Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana, Baton Rouge, New Orleans and the Northshore · August 11, 2016 to August 24, 2016

Against today's NWS flood stages, 32 of the 107 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (18 at major, 9 at moderate, 5 at minor). For 39 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Amite River at Magnolia, Amite River near Denham Springs, Comite River near Comite, Tchefuncte River near Covington, Tangipahoa River at Robert, Tickfaw River at Montpelier and 33 more. The highest tide gauge reading was 2.22 ft above MHHW at Freshwater Canal Locks (Aug 13, 2016 at 9:48 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 11, 2016 and August 24, 2016 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana, Baton Rouge, New Orleans and the Northshore. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
