Hurricane Laura (2020): peak water levels

56 river and stream gauges and 10 tide gauges · Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana · August 25, 2020 to August 31, 2020

Against today's NWS flood stages, 7 of the 56 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (1 at major, 1 at moderate, 5 at minor). For 2 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Vermilion Bay near Cypremort Point, Mouth of Atchafalaya River at Atchafalaya Bay. The highest tide gauge reading was 9.24 ft above MHHW at Calcasieu Pass (Aug 27, 2020 at 1:00 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 25, 2020 and August 31, 2020 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
