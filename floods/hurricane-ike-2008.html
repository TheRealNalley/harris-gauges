115 river and stream gauges, 5 Army Corps gauges, 125 Harris County gauges, 4 lakes and 9 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana · September 11, 2008 to September 18, 2008

Against today's NWS flood stages, 14 of the 115 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (2 at major, 4 at moderate, 8 at minor).
