Hurricane Ike (2008): peak water levels

115 river and stream gauges and 13 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana · September 11, 2008 to September 18, 2008

Against today's NWS flood stages, 13 of the 115 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (2 at major, 4 at moderate, 7 at minor). For 4 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Sabine River (at Navy Pier) at Orange, Hunting Bayou at Hoffman St, Houston, Buffalo Bayou at Turning Basin, Houston, Calcasieu River at I-10 at Lake Charles. The highest tide gauge reading was 10.93 ft above MHHW at Port Arthur (Sep 13, 2008 at 4:12 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between September 11, 2008 and September 18, 2008 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana, Lafayette and Acadiana. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
