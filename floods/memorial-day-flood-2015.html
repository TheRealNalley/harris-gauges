Memorial Day Flood (2015): peak water levels

75 river and stream gauges and 8 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston · May 24, 2015 to May 30, 2015

Against today's NWS flood stages, 19 of the 75 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (3 at major, 11 at moderate, 5 at minor). For 1 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Brays Bayou at Gessner Dr, Houston. How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between May 24, 2015 and May 30, 2015 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026.
