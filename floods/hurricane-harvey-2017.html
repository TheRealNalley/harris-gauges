Hurricane Harvey (2017): peak water levels

145 river and stream gauges and 32 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Victoria and Matagorda Bay, Corpus Christi and the Coastal Bend, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana · August 25, 2017 to September 8, 2017

Against today's NWS flood stages, 67 of the 145 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (51 at major, 8 at moderate, 8 at minor). For 62 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Spring Creek near Tomball, Cypress Creek at Katy-Hockley Rd near Hockley, Willow Creek near Tomball, Cypress Creek at Grant Rd near Cypress, West Fork San Jacinto River near Conroe, Spring Creek near Spring and 56 more. The highest tide gauge reading was 10.47 ft above MHHW at Manchester (Aug 29, 2017 at 4:54 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 25, 2017 and September 8, 2017 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Victoria and Matagorda Bay, Corpus Christi and the Coastal Bend, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026.
