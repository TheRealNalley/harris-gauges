Tropical Storm Imelda (2019): peak water levels

113 river and stream gauges and 16 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana · September 17, 2019 to September 25, 2019

Against today's NWS flood stages, 17 of the 113 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (9 at major, 2 at moderate, 6 at minor). For 3 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Peach Creek at Splendora, Cow Bayou near Mauriceville, Greens Bayou at Ley Rd, Houston. The highest tide gauge reading was 3.50 ft above MHHW at Manchester (Sep 19, 2019 at 2:48 PM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between September 17, 2019 and September 25, 2019 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston, Beaumont–Port Arthur, Lake Charles and Southwest Louisiana. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
