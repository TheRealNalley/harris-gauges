Tropical Storm Allison (2001): peak water levels

48 river and stream gauges and 5 tide gauges · Houston–Galveston · June 5, 2001 to June 14, 2001

Against today's NWS flood stages, 4 of the 48 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (1 at major, 1 at moderate, 2 at minor). For 4 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Brickhouse Gully at Costa Rica St, Houston, Garners Bayou near Humble, Little White Oak Bayou at Trimble St, Houston, Vince Bayou at Pasadena. The highest tide gauge reading was 6.27 ft above MHHW at Manchester (Jun 9, 2001 at 4:54 AM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between June 5, 2001 and June 14, 2001 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
