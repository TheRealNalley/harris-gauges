How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 16, 1983 and August 23, 1983 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS, NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Houston–Galveston. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's gauges are the peaks the county lists for this flood on each gauge's own page, and the rain its rain gauges recorded, from harriscountyfws.org. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
