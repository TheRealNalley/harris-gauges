Hurricane Ida (2021): peak water levels

102 river and stream gauges and 9 tide gauges · Baton Rouge, Houma–Thibodaux, New Orleans and the Northshore, Mississippi Gulf Coast · August 28, 2021 to September 4, 2021

Against today's NWS flood stages, 28 of the 102 river and stream gauges here peaked at or above a flood stage (5 at major, 11 at moderate, 12 at minor). For 14 of them it is the highest peak in their comparable USGS record: Tchoutacabouffa River near D'iberville, Pearl River at I-10 near Slidell, Delacroix Canal near Caernarvon, Crooked Bayou northwest of Lake Cuatro Caballo near Delacroix, Davis Pond Freshwater Diversion near Boutte, Empire Waterway south of Empire and 8 more. The highest tide gauge reading was 6.91 ft above MHHW at Bay Waveland Yacht Club (Aug 29, 2021 at 10:54 PM CDT).

How this page is built

Each gauge's highest reading between August 28, 2021 and September 4, 2021 (the window runs past the rain because large rivers crest days later), from USGS and NOAA records, for the gauges this site carries today in Baton Rouge, Houma–Thibodaux, New Orleans and the Northshore, Mississippi Gulf Coast. Gauges installed since are not listed; a gauge out of service at the time has no row. Harris County's own gauges are not included: this site has their readings only from September 2026. Readings only: this page says nothing about damage or what happened at any street or property.
