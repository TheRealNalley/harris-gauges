How to read these numbers

Each figure is the Flood Warning System's own rolling 24-hour rainfall total at the gauge, read every 15 minutes. A day's figure is the highest of those totals during that day (Central time), so a storm that straddles midnight can count toward two days. Rain gauges can clog, fail or over-read in wind; nothing here is verified independently, and a rainfall total says nothing about flooding at any street or property.
