How to read these numbers

Level is the water surface elevation the gauge reports, not a depth. Below bank is the distance from that level to the top of the channel at the gauge, using the top-of-bank figure Fort Bend County publishes with each reading. 7-day closest is the smallest that distance has been over the last week. History on this site starts September 15, 2026, so ranges and comparisons fill in over the coming weeks.
