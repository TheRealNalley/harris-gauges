The Corps reads stage where USGS and NOAA don't: on the lower Mississippi, in the Atchafalaya basin, at locks, and on both sides of floodgates. Each gauge reads on its own datum (NAVD88, the older NGVD29, or a local zero), so readings can't be compared from one gauge to the next. Corps gauges within 1 km of a USGS gauge already on this site are left out. How this site works
