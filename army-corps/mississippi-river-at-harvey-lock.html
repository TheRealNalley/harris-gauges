How to read this gauge

The number is the water surface elevation in feet on NGVD29, an older national vertical datum (NGVD29 and NAVD88 heights differ by an amount that varies from place to place): a height above a fixed survey reference, not a depth. Readings on different datums can't be compared directly.

Only the gauge's current, unbroken run of years is used: years before a gap or a sudden shift in level (a likely change of datum) are left out, since they may not be on the same scale. A small change of datum would not show, so treat older years as approximate. Readings the record doesn't back up are left out: a lone spike, a placeholder value, or a stretch where the sensor plainly failed.
