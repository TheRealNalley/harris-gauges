How to read this gauge

The number is the stage in feet above the gauge's own zero: a height on this gauge's own scale, not a depth, and not comparable with other gauges.

The flood stages are the National Weather Service's references for this location, on the same scale as the reading. They describe the gauge, not any particular street or property. The flood-fight phases are the Corps' own levels for patrolling and reinforcing the levees.
